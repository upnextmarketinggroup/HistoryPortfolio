About Me

Five objects that explain where I come from

EXHIBIT 01

Boxing Gloves

2017 · Glendale, California

These boxing gloves belong to me. They were made by Revgear, and my dad gave them to me in 2017 after I signed up for my first boxing class in Glendale, California. They mark the start of a sport that became one of the biggest parts of my life for the next eight years. I have kept them ever since as a reminder of where that journey began.

EXHIBIT 02

High School Diploma

June 2026 · La Crescenta, California

This high school diploma belongs to me and was issued by Clark Magnet High School in June of 2026. The school is located in La Crescenta, California. It matters to me because it marks the end of my childhood and my leap into adulthood. I received it directly at my graduation ceremony.

EXHIBIT 03

Sony FX3 Camera

November 2025 · Burbank, California

This camera belongs to me. It is a Sony FX3, which I bought in November of 2025 at the Best Buy in the Empire Center in Burbank, California. It matters to me because it symbolizes the career I want to go into, marketing, and my creative side. I purchased it myself.

EXHIBIT 04

6th Grade Camp Polaroid

January 2020 · Lake Arrowhead, California

This polaroid belongs to me and was taken by a camp counselor in January of 2020 at Camp Pali in Lake Arrowhead, California. It matters to me because it was the last time I was with that group of friends before COVID hit. I have kept it in a photo album ever since.

EXHIBIT 05

Mt. Lukens Summit

March 2026 · Angeles National Forest, California

This photo belongs to me and was taken by a friend on March 28, 2026, at the summit of Mt. Lukens in the Angeles National Forest near La Crescenta, California. It matters to me because it symbolizes my hobby of hiking and marks the longest hike I have done, thirteen miles round trip. My friend took the photo on my phone, and it has stayed saved there ever since.
