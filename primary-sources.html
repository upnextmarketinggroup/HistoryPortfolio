Primary Sources

Two closer looks at objects from my own life

SOURCE 01

High School Diploma

A closer look at the document that marked the end of my time as a student, and the start of everything after it.

Type
Other, a certificate
Description
The diploma is a formal printed certificate with old-style lettering stating that I completed my coursework at Anderson W. Clark Magnet High School in Glendale, California, dated June 11, 2026, carrying three signatures and two gold seals plus the official district seal.
Made by
The Glendale Unified School District, the Board of Education President, the Superintendent of Schools, and the Principal, Mark Rubio.
Made for
Me, as the graduating student.
Date
June 11, 2026
Place
Glendale, California
Summary
This document is an official certificate from Glendale Unified School District confirming that I completed the required coursework and graduated from Clark Magnet High School in June 2026.
What it shows
My completion of high school, formally recognizing that I finished the coursework required to graduate.
Why it was made
The school and district wrote it to formally certify that I met the graduation requirements and to give me an official record I can use for colleges or employers.
Evidence
“Has completed in a satisfactory manner the Course of Study prescribed for Graduation from this School and is therefore awarded this Diploma.”
Historical context
Around the time I graduated, SpaceX went public in a record breaking IPO, making Elon Musk the world’s first trillionaire.
What this reveals
This document tells me the exact date my graduation became official and exactly which school officials signed off on it.
Further evidence
I’d look at my official transcript and photos from the graduation ceremony itself.

SOURCE 02

6th Grade Camp Polaroid

A candid photo from the last normal week before the world changed.

Type
Other, a photograph
Description
It’s a color photo, vertical and rectangular, showing me sitting down alone in the shot.
Made by
A camp counselor took the photo.
Made for
Just for me, to keep.
Date
January 2020
Place
Camp Pali, Lake Arrowhead, California
Summary
This photo is a portrait of me sitting on the grass during 6th grade camp in January 2020.
What it shows
The photo shows just me, sitting on the grass and smiling into the camera.
Why it was made
The counselor took it as a random, candid moment during camp, not for any official reason.
Evidence
My expression in the photo, happy and carefree, still very much a kid, shows this was just a casual, unposed moment.
Historical context
In January 2020, COVID-19 was just starting to spread in China and the first US case had barely been confirmed.
What this reveals
From this photo, I can see exactly what I looked like and what I was wearing that specific day.
Further evidence
I’d look at other photos from the same camp trip and early January 2020 news coverage of COVID first appearing.
